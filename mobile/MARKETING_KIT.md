# Ascend: Daily Discipline - Marketing Kit

## Positioning

Ascend: Daily Discipline is a 5-minute daily discipline app: short lessons, quizzes, streaks, and practical actions across 5 paths.

Core line:
```
Discipline, one lesson a day.
```

TR line:
```
Disiplin, her gun tek ders.
```

## Target Audience

Primary:
- 22-35
- Wants better focus, routine, self-control, and less phone-driven distraction
- Likes habit systems, streaks, stoicism, digital detox, and productivity

Secondary:
- Founders, freelancers, students, and tech workers
- Wants a structured discipline curriculum instead of generic motivation

## Initial Test Plan

Run small tests before scaling spend:

- Apple Search Ads exact keywords: discipline, habit, focus, routine.
- TikTok/Reels founder demo: app walkthrough, no stock footage.
- Screenshot A/B: first screenshot lesson-first vs streak-first.

Watch:
- Impression -> product page rate
- Product page -> download rate
- Download -> first lesson completion
- Paywall view -> purchase_started
- purchase_started -> purchase_unlocked

## 7-Day Growth Experiment

Budget suggestion:
- Apple Search Ads: $10-20/day
- TikTok/Reels: 2-3 organic posts/day

Success signal:
- Product page conversion stays above 15%
- First lesson completion above 50%
- Purchase started visible in analytics
- At least one source produces paid conversion or strong first-lesson completion
